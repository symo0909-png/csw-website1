What we do

Fabrication & construction services

From concept and detailing through fabrication, delivery and installation — built with high-grade materials and checked at every stage.

01

Structural Steel Fabrication

Design, fabrication and erection of steel structures for commercial and industrial use.

  • Steel sheds & covered structures
  • Canopies & carports
  • Staircases, handrails & balustrades
  • Platforms, walkways & equipment bases
02

Mezzanine Floors

Add usable floor area to your warehouse or showroom without the cost of extending the building.

  • Steel frame design & fabrication
  • Chequered plate, grating or timber decks
  • Integrated stairs & safety railing
  • Load calculations & drawings on request
03

Warehouse Construction

Turnkey steel warehouses built for durability, cost efficiency and UAE building compliance.

  • Pre-engineered steel frames
  • Roof sheeting & wall cladding
  • Sliding & roller shutter doors
  • Louvers, skylights & civil coordination
04

Portable Cabins & Site Offices

Insulated steel-frame cabins with sandwich panel cladding, built for rapid deployment.

  • Site offices & project cabins
  • Accommodation & rest units
  • Guard rooms & security booths
  • Storage & multi-room modular blocks
05

Fit-Out & Interior Steel Works

Precision metalwork for fit-out contractors, retail, hospitality and commercial spaces.

  • Steel partitions, frames & dividers
  • Ceiling grids & cladding frames
  • Feature staircases & balustrades
  • Outdoor seating & pergola enclosures
06

Piping & Pipe Spool Fabrication

Stainless and carbon steel pipe spools fabricated to client isometrics and tight tolerances.

  • SS & CS pipe spool fabrication
  • Flanged assemblies, tees & reducers
  • Process, fire & water service spools
  • Pressure testing & inspection
